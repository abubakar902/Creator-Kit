What can you customize?

  • Add, duplicate and remove multiple text layers.
  • Choose fonts, text size, bold, italic, alignment and letter spacing.
  • Use solid colours, two-colour gradients, outlines and drop shadows.
  • Add coloured text backgrounds and adjust their opacity.
  • Move text around the photo and rotate it to suit your design.
  • Start quickly with headline, neon, minimal and quote presets.
  • Download the finished design as a PNG image.

How to add text to a photo

  1. Open the Photo Text Editor and choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP photo.
  2. Add a text layer and type your title, caption or quote.
  3. Customize the font, colour, gradient, outline, shadow and position.
  4. Drag the text directly on the photo to place it where you want.
  5. Download your edited photo as a PNG.

Make your photos stand out

Use the editor to create social media graphics, photo captions, quote images, event announcements, simple posters and personalized pictures. For best results, use a clear photo and keep important text away from the edges.
