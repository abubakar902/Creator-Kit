Projects & Media

Set up a project and bring your files into the editor.

＋Create a Project & Choose Settings

What it does: Creates the video canvas that all your clips, text and overlays will use.

  1. Open Video Studio and choose Create New Project.
  2. Enter a project name.
  3. Choose an aspect ratio: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts/Reels, 1:1 for square posts, 4:5 for portrait posts, or Custom.
  4. For Custom, enter a width and height from 240 to 3,840 pixels.
  5. Choose a resolution: 720p, Full HD (1080p), 2K or 4K.
  6. Choose 24, 30 or 60 FPS, then click Create Project.
Tip: Match the project shape to the place where you will post. Use 9:16 for vertical Shorts and Reels.
▤Open, Save & Manage Projects

What it does: Keeps your work available from the Video Studio project page.

  1. Return to the Studio dashboard and find Open Existing Project.
  2. Choose Open Editor beside a saved project.
  3. The editor saves changes automatically. Use Save to save immediately.
  4. Use Undo and Redo if you make an unwanted change.
  5. Use Delete only when you really want to remove a project and its saved media.
Projects are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, using a different browser or moving to another device can make local projects unavailable. Project deletion also removes the media saved with that project.
⇧Import Videos, Images & Audio

What it does: Adds files from your device to Project Assets so you can place them on the timeline.

  1. Open a project and click Choose files under Project Assets.
  2. Select one or more video, image or audio files.
  3. Imported files appear in the media list.
  4. Click the Add button beside a file to place it at the current playhead.
  5. Videos go to Video, images to Overlay, and audio to Audio by default. You can move visual clips between Video and Overlay.
Each imported file must be 750 MB or smaller. The browser must support the file's media format to preview and process it.
◫Create a Lightweight Video Proxy

What it does: Creates a smaller preview copy of a video to make timeline playback smoother on slower devices.

  1. Find a video in Project Assets.
  2. Click Create proxy and let it finish.
  3. Use Use proxy for smoother preview, or Use original for full-quality preview.
A proxy is only for preview. Video export always uses your original imported file, so the proxy does not lower final export quality.

Preview & Timeline

Understand the editor screen and arrange your video.

▶Preview, Fit, Fill & Guides

What it does: Shows the frame at the current playhead position.

  1. Press Play to preview or Stop to return to the beginning.
  2. Choose Fit to see the full clip inside the preview, or Fill to cover the preview area.
  3. Click Guides to show safe-area and composition lines. Click it again to hide them.
  4. Drag a selected video, image, sticker or text on the preview to change its position. Use the corner handle to resize items that show it.
Guides are only visual helpers. They are not included in the exported video.
▥Timeline Tracks & Clip Controls

What it does: Controls when each video, image, text, audio or caption appears.

  • Video / Image: Main video clips and image clips.
  • Overlay: Extra videos, images and stickers placed above the main picture. For picture-in-picture, add a second video or image here, then resize and position it in Preview or Properties.
  • Text: Text layers and titles.
  • Audio: Music, sound effects, detached video audio and generated voice-over clips.
  • Captions: Timed subtitles created by Auto Captions.
  1. Drag a clip to move it along the timeline.
  2. Drag either end of a clip to trim its duration.
  3. Drag the pink playhead to seek. Click the ruler to move to a time.
  4. Click a clip to open its Properties.
  5. Use Split at playhead to cut the selected clip at the pink line.
  6. Use Duplicate or Delete for the selected clip.
⌖Snap, Zoom & Fit All

Snap On helps clips line up neatly with nearby clip edges and timeline points.

  1. Click Snap On to turn magnetic snapping on or off.
  2. Use the Zoom slider to see more detail or more of the timeline.
  3. Click Fit All to fit the whole project duration into the timeline view.
Press N to toggle snapping while you are not typing in an input field.

Clip Editing & Properties

Select a clip first. Its available controls appear in the Properties panel.

⚙Name & Timing

What it does: Changes the clip name, its start time and its duration.

  1. Select a clip on the timeline.
  2. Open Name & Timing in Properties.
  3. Change the clip name, Start (seconds) or Duration (seconds).
  4. Check the preview and timeline, then click Apply.
Start controls when the clip appears. Duration controls how long it stays on screen or plays.
⤢Transform, Position & Crop / Resize

What it does: Changes the size, position, direction and framing of a clip.

  1. Select a video or image clip and open Transform & Position.
  2. Adjust Scale, Rotation, Position X and Position Y.
  3. Use Flip Horizontal or Flip Vertical to mirror the clip.
  4. Choose Fit to show the whole clip, Fill to crop the edges, or Stretch to fill the canvas.
  5. Lower Opacity to make the clip transparent, then click Apply.
You can also drag the selected visual clip in the Preview to position it.
✦Effects & Clip Transitions

What it does: Changes the look of a visual clip and adds an entrance effect.

  1. Select a video or image clip.
  2. Open Effects & Transitions.
  3. Choose a preset: Original, Cinematic, Vivid, Warm, Cool, Black & White or Vintage.
  4. Fine-tune Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Blur and Color tone.
  5. Choose an entrance transition: Fade In, Zoom In, Slide from Left, Wipe from Left or Blur Reveal.
  6. Set its duration and click Apply.
The clip entrance transition plays when the playhead reaches the start of that clip.
⏩Speed Ramp

What it does: Changes clip speed smoothly from its start to its end.

  1. Select a video clip and open Speed Ramp, Chroma Key & Masking.
  2. Choose a Start speed and End speed (0.25× to 4×).
  3. For slow motion, choose a lower speed. For fast motion, choose a higher speed.
  4. Use different start and end speeds for a gradual speed change, then click Apply.
The clip duration adjusts automatically to keep the full selected source section.
◩Chroma Key / Green Screen

What it does: Makes a chosen background colour transparent.

  1. Select a video or image clip and open Speed Ramp, Chroma Key & Masking.
  2. Turn on Enable Chroma Key.
  3. Choose the background colour with the colour picker.
  4. Increase Colour tolerance to remove more shades of that colour.
  5. Adjust Edge softness to smooth the cut-out edges.
  6. Place a background clip underneath on Video or Overlay and click Apply.
A green or blue background works best when the colour is even and does not appear on the subject.
◒Clip Masks

What it does: Shows only a selected shape or part of a video or image.

  1. Select a visual clip and choose a shape in Clip mask shape.
  2. Choose Circle, Ellipse, Rounded corners, Left half, Right half or Horizontal adjustable mask.
  3. For the horizontal mask, drag TOP or BOTTOM to move one edge.
  4. Drag the centre handle to move both lines together. Drag ↻ to rotate, or use the angle slider.
  5. Adjust Line softness and click Apply.
Masks are included in the final export.

Smart Editing Tools

Automatic editing helpers inside the Properties panel.

◫Detect Scenes

What it does: Looks for scene changes in a selected video and splits the clip at detected cuts.

  1. Select a video clip on the timeline.
  2. Open Smart Editing Tools in Properties.
  3. Click Detect Scenes and let the scan finish.
  4. Review the new clip sections and adjust any split that you do not need.
Choose a video clip at least 2 seconds long. Very similar scenes or fast camera movement can affect detection.
♫Sync to Beats

What it does: Detects beats in music and moves nearby visual clips towards matching beats.

  1. Select an audio or video clip that contains music.
  2. Click Sync to Beats in Smart Editing Tools.
  3. Wait for beat detection.
  4. Review the visual clips that moved and adjust their timing if needed.
Use a clear music track at normal speed. Clips move only when a nearby beat is available and the move will not overlap another clip.
◖Remove Silence

What it does: Finds quiet parts in an audio or video clip, keeps the sound sections and closes the gaps.

  1. Select an audio clip or a video clip that has sound.
  2. Set Silence threshold. A higher value removes more quiet sound; a lower value keeps softer speech.
  3. Choose Speech edge padding to keep a little space before and after speech.
  4. Click Remove Silence and review the new sections.
For safe cutting, use a normal-speed clip without keyframes. Other clips must not overlap its timeline range. If video audio is detached, select the Audio clip instead.
◉AI Colour Correction

What it does: Analyses a frame from a selected video or image and suggests subtle brightness, contrast, saturation and colour-tone corrections.

  1. Select a video or image clip.
  2. Click AI Colour Correction.
  3. Review the applied result in Preview.
  4. Open Effects & Transitions to fine-tune the correction, or use Undo to return to the previous look.
This feature needs an internet connection and available AI service. It applies a starting correction, not a guaranteed perfect grade for every scene.
文Translate Captions

What it does: Translates captions that are already on the Captions track into another language.

  1. Generate captions first, or add caption clips to the Captions track.
  2. Choose a target language in Translate captions to.
  3. Click Translate Captions.
  4. Review the translated lines in Preview and edit any names or words that need correction.
Translation changes the existing caption text. Download a copy of your SRT first if you want to keep the original wording separately.

Free Local Video Tools

These tools process media in your browser on your device.

First use: Some tools need to load a processing engine or AI model before they start. Keep the editor tab open while processing. The original imported media remains in Project Assets.
↶Reverse Video

What it does: Creates a reversed copy of the selected video clip.

  1. Select a video clip.
  2. Choose whether to turn on Also reverse audio. It is off by default so speech is not played backwards.
  3. Click Reverse Video and wait for the new reversed clip.
  4. Preview the result. The original file stays in Project Assets.
The selected section must be 30 seconds or shorter and the source file must be 50 MB or smaller. Split a longer clip first.
◎Motion Tracking

What it does: Detects a person or visible object and adds keyframes so the selected video follows its movement.

  1. Select a video clip.
  2. Choose Person or Any visible object as the tracking target.
  3. Click Motion Tracking and let the scan finish.
  4. Play the clip and review the automatic movement. You can adjust the keyframes if needed.
Use a bright clip where the subject is easy to see. The clip should be at least 1.2 seconds long.
♫Enhance Audio

What it does: Reduces steady background noise in an audio clip.

  1. Select a clip on the Audio track.
  2. For sound inside a video, select the video and click Detach Audio first.
  3. Select the new Audio clip and click Enhance Audio.
  4. Preview the processed clip and adjust its volume if needed.
The source audio file must be 80 MB or smaller and under 3 minutes. The selected section can be up to 2 minutes. This is noise reduction, not full music mastering.
✂Remove Person Background

What it does: Removes the background around a person in a selected video, creating a person cut-out preview.

  1. Select a video clip.
  2. Click Remove Person BG to turn the person cut-out on.
  3. Place another video or image underneath on the Video or Overlay track to use it as a background.
  4. Click the button again to turn the effect off.
This tool detects people only, not every kind of object. Transparent areas show the clip below or the dark canvas if there is no background layer.
▣Auto Reframe

What it does: Follows the detected person and automatically adjusts the framing as they move.

  1. Select a video clip with one clearly visible person.
  2. Set your project to the target shape, such as 9:16 for a vertical Short or Reel.
  3. Click Auto Reframe.
  4. Review the framing and adjust keyframes if the subject moves out of view.
Auto Reframe follows a person. It works best when the person stays visible and is not hidden for long periods.

Text & Animation

Add titles, style them and animate movement.

TAdd Text

What it does: Adds a text layer at the current playhead position.

  1. Move the playhead to where the title should begin.
  2. Click + Text above the timeline.
  3. Enter your words in the prompt and confirm.
  4. The text clip appears on the Text track. Select it to edit its style and timing.
A new text clip starts with a 5-second duration. You can trim it on the timeline or change Duration in Properties.
AaText & Style

What it does: Changes how a text layer or caption looks.

  1. Select a text or caption clip.
  2. Open Text & Style in Properties.
  3. Change the words, text colour, font size, font, weight, alignment and background.
  4. Adjust background opacity, italic style, rotation and text opacity.
  5. Choose a text effect: None, Soft shadow, Glow, Neon or Bold outline.
  6. Choose an entrance: Fade in, Pop in, Slide up or Bounce in.
  7. Click Apply and check the Preview.
You can drag the text on Preview to move it and use its corner handle to resize it.
◆Keyframes & Animation

What it does: Animates a visual clip or text by saving its position, size, rotation and opacity at different times.

  1. Select a video, image or text clip.
  2. Move the playhead to the first animation point and click Add keyframe.
  3. Move the playhead to another time and change the item's position, scale or other supported values. The next keyframe can be created automatically.
  4. Use Previous and Next to jump between keyframes.
  5. Use Copy and Paste to reuse a keyframe.
  6. Choose Linear, Ease in, Ease out or Ease in & out for the motion between points.
Add two or more keyframes to create movement. Check the clip from before the first keyframe to after the last one.

Audio Editing

Control sound, clean up noise and balance your mix.

♫Audio Editor: Volume, Fades, EQ & Balance

What it does: Adjusts the loudness and tone of a selected Audio clip.

  1. Select an Audio clip on the timeline.
  2. Open Audio Editor in Properties.
  3. Use Volume to make it quieter or louder.
  4. Set Fade in and Fade out times to make the sound start or end smoothly.
  5. Adjust Bass, Mid and Treble to shape the sound.
  6. Use Balance to move sound towards the left or right channel.
  7. Click Apply, play the video and listen again.
EQ and stereo balance affect playback and video export. Start with small changes and listen on headphones.
⇢Detach Audio from a Video

What it does: Extracts a video's sound into its own Audio track clip so you can edit it separately.

  1. Select the video clip.
  2. Click Detach Audio in Properties.
  3. Wait for extraction to finish. A separate clip appears on the Audio track.
  4. Select that Audio clip to change volume, fades, EQ, balance or use Enhance Audio.
After detaching, the video's built-in sound is turned off. Use the separate Audio clip to control it.
For reliable extraction, use a video under 10 minutes and 300 MB.
CC

Captions & Subtitles

Create timed subtitles, edit them and download an SRT file.

CCAI Auto Captions

What it does: Listens to a selected video or audio clip and creates timed subtitle clips.

  1. Select a video or audio clip that contains speech.
  2. Click Auto Captions above the timeline.
  3. Choose a spoken language or Auto detect.
  4. Click Generate captions and wait for the transcript.
  5. Captions appear on the Captions track. Select a caption to edit its words, style, position or timing.
  6. Click Download SRT in the captions window to save subtitle text and timestamps.
The selected clip's audio is sent securely to the AI transcription service to create the captions. Only generate captions when you are ready to process that audio.
Clear speech gives better results. Check names, numbers and punctuation before exporting.
文Translate Existing Captions

What it does: Changes existing caption text into another language while keeping the same caption timing.

  1. Generate captions first.
  2. Choose the target language from Translate captions to.
  3. Click Translate Captions inside Smart Editing Tools.
  4. Review the translated captions and make corrections if needed.
Available target languages include English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Turkish, Bengali, Chinese (Simplified) and Japanese.

Stickers, Shapes & Voice-over

Add visual decorations or create a spoken voice clip.

✦Stickers & Shapes

What it does: Adds a graphic or emoji to the Overlay track.

  1. Move the playhead to where the sticker should begin.
  2. Click Stickers & Shapes above the timeline.
  3. Choose a shape or emoji and select its colour.
  4. Click Add to timeline.
  5. Drag the sticker in Preview to move it. Use the corner handle to resize it.
  6. Trim or move the clip on the Overlay track to set when it appears.
Available designs include Star, Heart, Circle, Rounded rectangle, Arrow, Speech bubble, Lightning bolt, Check mark, and five emoji stickers. You can add more than one image sticker.
♫AI Voice-over

What it does: Turns your written words into an MP3 voice clip and adds it to the Audio track.

  1. Move the playhead to the point where the voice should begin.
  2. Click AI Voice-over.
  3. Choose one of the available voices.
  4. Type up to 2,000 characters.
  5. Confirm that you want to generate the voice-over, then click Generate & add to timeline.
  6. The MP3 is added to the Audio track at the playhead. Select it to adjust volume, fades and EQ.
Voices: Angus, Asteria, Arcas, Orion, Orpheus, Athena, Luna, Zeus, Perseus, Helios, Hera and Stella. Generation needs an internet connection and may be subject to AI service or account usage limits.

Export & Export Queue

Save the finished video to your device.

⇩Export a Video

What it does: Renders your timeline into a video file that you can download.

  1. Click Export in the top bar.
  2. Choose MP4 for broad compatibility or WebM for a smaller web-friendly file.
  3. Choose resolution: 480p, 720p, 1080p or Project resolution.
  4. Choose the frame rate: Project FPS, 24, 30 or 60 FPS.
  5. Choose Smaller file, Standard or High quality.
  6. Click Start export. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
  7. Your finished video downloads automatically. Use Download again to save it again while the export window is open.
For a first test, export a short section at 720p. Higher resolution and quality need more time and memory.
When the browser cannot record MP4 directly, the local MP4 fallback supports up to 720p, projects up to 2 minutes and temporary files up to 100 MB. If MP4 is not available, choose WebM. Native MP4 support may allow different limits.
☷Export Queue

What it does: Runs several export settings one after another, such as 720p and 1080p versions.

  1. Open Export and choose the first set of settings.
  2. Click Add current settings.
  3. Change the format, resolution, frame rate or quality and add another job.
  4. Repeat for the versions you need, up to 8 jobs.
  5. Click Start Queue. Each completed job downloads automatically.
Keep the browser tab open until every queued job is finished. If one job fails, the queue pauses and the remaining jobs stay listed.

Quick Troubleshooting

Try these steps when something does not work as expected.

The preview is blank or a video will not play

Check that the file type is supported by your browser. Try an MP4 video in an updated Chrome or Edge browser, re-import the file, and make sure the clip is on the Video or Overlay track.

Preview playback is slow or stutters

Create a lightweight proxy for the video in Project Assets and choose Use proxy. Close other heavy browser tabs and try editing a shorter clip.

I cannot hear the video's sound

The video may have detached audio. Find its Audio clip on the Audio track, select it, check its Volume setting and make sure the playhead is inside the audio clip.

Auto Captions did not create subtitles

Check that the selected video or audio contains clear speech, choose the correct spoken language, and make sure the clip has sound. Try a shorter section if processing takes too long.

Motion Tracking or Auto Reframe cannot find the subject

Use a brighter clip with the person or object clearly visible. Motion Tracking can use Any visible object; Auto Reframe follows a person.

Reverse Video will not start

Check the selected clip is 30 seconds or shorter and the source file is 50 MB or smaller. Split a longer clip and try again.

MP4 is unavailable or export fails

Choose WebM, or try MP4 at 720p with a timeline under 2 minutes. Keep the editor tab open and try a smaller export if the browser runs out of memory.

I cannot find a saved project

Open Video Studio in the same browser and on the same device where you created it. Projects are stored locally and do not automatically move between devices.

A local tool is still processing

Keep the tab open. Some tools download a processing model the first time. Click the same local-tool button again to cancel; your original media remains in Project Assets.
