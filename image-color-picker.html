How to use this tool

  1. Choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Click the exact point on the image whose colour you want to identify.
  3. View the selected colour swatch, HEX code and RGB code.
  4. Use Copy HEX or Copy RGB to copy the value.

What you can do with this tool

  • Pick a colour from any visible point in an image.
  • Get HEX and RGB values for design and web projects.
  • Preview the selected colour in a large swatch.
  • Copy colour codes with one click.
  • Process images locally in your browser; the image is not sent to our server.

Frequently asked questions

What is an image color picker?

An image color picker identifies the colour at a point you click on a photo and shows its HEX and RGB values.

Which image formats are supported?

PNG, JPG and WebP images up to 10 MB are supported.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to TrivexaAI servers.
