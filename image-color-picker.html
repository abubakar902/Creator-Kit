How to use this tool

Frequently asked questions

What is an image color picker?

An image color picker reads the pixel colour at a selected point and can show its HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK values.

Which image formats are supported?

PNG, JPG and WebP images up to 10 MB are supported.

Can I make a palette from my image?

Yes. Generate a palette with 5, 8 or 10 approximate dominant colours, then download it as a PNG or text file.

Can I zoom in to pick a pixel?

Yes. Use the zoom buttons and the magnifier preview to inspect image details.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to TrivexaAI servers.
